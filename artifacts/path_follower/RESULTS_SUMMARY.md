## Caveats to read these numbers with

- **The overall success rate is dominated by the randomly sampled
  curvature mixture, so it is not a stable statistic.** Measured on the
  same checkpoint at stage 6: 96.9% at seed 8206 and 81.6% at seed 4206,
  a 15 point swing. The reason is visible in the table above - the
  analytic prior scores 32% at k=0.25 and 100% at k=0.40, so a draw with
  relatively more gentle arcs scores much lower. Quote the
  (path type x curvature) rows, never the headline number.
- `split=train` is the distribution the policies were trained on. A run
  with no `test` row has no measured generalisation. The two sides are
  not comparable across runs unless both are present.
- Episode counts of 32-128 give roughly +/- 8-15 point confidence
  intervals; only compare runs at equal n. The rows below with n<64 and
  a large gap to a neighbouring run are usually noise.
- Successful arc episodes end at a median endpoint distance of about
  0.199 m against a 0.20 m threshold, so arc success is
  threshold-hugging rather than comfortably accurate.
- The failure mode depends on the regime, so pooled counts mislead.
  End-to-end PPO runs on stage 2 fail overwhelmingly by `timeout` (the
  robot never finishes), while stage-6 runs driven by the analytic prior
  fail almost exclusively by `timeout` as well, with `deviation`
  appearing only in the earlier residual runs.
